Free weekly cook-together · RMIT City

a free weekly cooking circle for students who just arrived in melbourne.

A 90-minute cooking circle led by a senior student from your part of the world. Rescued fresh produce, the staples you actually cook with, and 3–4 meals to take home. No forms about money. No queue.

Why we started this

running out of food money before the fortnight ends is common in first semester, and almost nobody talks about it.

The first months after arriving are the riskiest: new prices, first time cooking, casual shifts that don't cover rent and groceries (Waite et al. 2025). The numbers are from recent Australian studies.

52.7%
of surveyed University of Tasmania students were food insecure in 2024, up from 42.0% in 2022.Kent et al. 2025, Health Promotion Journal of Australia
61% vs 38%
of international and domestic students at the same university were food insecure in 2022 (University of Tasmania).Kent et al. 2022, Nutrients
45%
of food-insecure Australian households say embarrassment or shame is a barrier to seeking food relief. So we made it a cooking club, not a charity.Foodbank Hunger Report 2025

How it works

three steps, thirty seconds to start.

  1. 01

    Reserve a seat

    Tell us your campus, when you arrived and what you'd love to cook. We never ask about visas, income or work hours.

  2. 02

    Get the menu on Sunday

    Your mentor posts the week's dishes, the kitchen location and what to bring in the group chat (WhatsApp or WeChat, your choice).

  3. 03

    Cook Tuesday, eat all week

    90 minutes with 8–16 students and one mentor. Leave with 3–4 labelled boxes, a recipe card and people to sit with in class.

Reserve a seat

pilot circles are planned for october 2026 at rmit city.

Five questions. We use them only to plan the menu and the kitchen. Seats would be confirmed by message on the Sunday before.

Is it really free?

Yes during the pilot. We plan to cook with rescued produce plus a small staples budget; supply is still being arranged. If the circle continues, we would test a $2–3 co-payment per circle, never more.

I can't cook at all. Is that a problem?

That's the point. Every circle teaches one skill in ten minutes, and your mentor will be a senior student who once started from scratch too.

Who sees my answers?

Only the student project team. Your answers go to a private Google Sheet that only the team can open, and we delete them when the course ends in December 2026. We never ask about visa, income or work hours.
