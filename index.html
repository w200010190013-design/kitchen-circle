why we started this

running out of food money before the fortnight ends is common in first semester, and almost nobody talks about it.

The first months after arriving are the riskiest: new prices, first time cooking, casual shifts that don't cover rent and groceries (Waite et al. 2025). The numbers below are from recent Australian studies.

52.7%

of surveyed Australian university students were food insecure in 2024, up from 42.0% in 2022.

Kent et al. 2025, Health Promotion Journal of Australia
61% vs 34%

international students were almost twice as likely as domestic students to be food insecure.

Kent et al. 2022, Nutrients
45%

of food-insecure Australians say embarrassment or shame stops them asking for help. So we made it a cooking club, not a charity.

Foodbank Hunger Report 2025
how it works

three steps, thirty seconds to start.

01

reserve a seat

Tell us your campus, when you arrived and what you'd love to cook. We never ask about visas, income or work hours.

02

get the menu on sunday

Your mentor posts the week's dishes, the kitchen location and what to bring in the group chat (WhatsApp or WeChat, your choice).

03

cook tuesday, eat all week

90 minutes with 8–16 students and one mentor. Leave with 3–4 labelled boxes, a recipe card and people to sit with in class.

reserve a seat

pilot circles start in october 2026 at rmit city.

Five questions. We use them only to plan the menu and the kitchen. Seats are confirmed by message on Sunday.

is it really free?

Yes during the pilot. Produce is rescued food from Foodbank Victoria and SecondBite partners; staples are covered by a small pilot budget. If it continues, we are testing a $2–3 co-payment per circle, never more.

i can't cook at all. is that a problem?

That's the point. Every circle teaches one skill in ten minutes, and your mentor cooked the same dishes for the first time two years ago.

who sees my answers?

Only the student team running the pilot. We don't ask about visa, income or work hours, and sign-up data is deleted when the course ends in December 2026.

Evenings you could come (tick any)

senior students

become a food mentor.

Ninety minutes on a Tuesday. You bring one dish from home you can teach in ten minutes; we bring the ingredients, the kitchen and the people. Volunteer certificate for your CV; a small stipend is part of what the pilot is testing.
